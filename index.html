A city-building lab on urbanization

The Urban Game: Chicago

Start with empty prairie in 1837. Build a city by 1900.

  1. Each round opens with a short piece of history.
  2. Then you are told what must be built. You decide where it goes.
  3. Then you see what your choices did to the people who live there.
Round clock If the clock runs out, the builders finish the job wherever they like.
Graphics High adds shadows, street lamps and wires, and more detailed people. On a Chromebook, use Medium or Low.

Click to build. Drag to move the map. Scroll to zoom. Teacher script
